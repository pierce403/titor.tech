### Useful items

- `S01:B01` — `fact` — Locator: section, page, paragraph, timestamp, or commit
  Concise original-language paraphrase of the useful fact.

- `S01:B02` — `quote` — Locator: section, page, paragraph, timestamp, or commit
  “Short exact quotation.”

- `S01:B03` — `counterpoint` — Locator: section, page, paragraph, timestamp, or commit
  Evidence that limits or challenges the proposed thesis.
